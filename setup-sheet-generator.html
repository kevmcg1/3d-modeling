Stock with jaws reversed
Stock sits on step

Steps facing in hold the stock on its outside (front step = largest diameter). Facing out, the jaws are reversed: either a ring sits over them and they grip its bore (front step = smallest bore), or a solid bar sits inside them and they clamp its outside (the step sets how deep it sits). Pick a step and the stock moves onto it.

Stick-out runs to the finished face from the point you pick: the right of the jaws (or collet), the right of a step, or the chuck face. With a collet, "Right of Step" gives the stock's overall length. The origin is on the spindle centerline at the finished face (X0 Z0).
